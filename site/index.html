Allowed

  • Encrypted full-margin counterfactual evaluation under the declared canary.
  • Client-side stable action selection and client-side OPE division.
  • Exact Concrete integer agreement for bounded canaries.
  • Approximate CKKS execution under a separate polynomial identifier and error evidence.

Forbidden

  • No private training or malicious-server integrity.
  • No indefinite-horizon safety or policy-optimality claim.
  • No first predictive shield or first private OPE claim.
  • No support, unbiasedness, or causal identification by encryption alone.
